# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, respect privacy, and welcome contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@djai.academy`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
