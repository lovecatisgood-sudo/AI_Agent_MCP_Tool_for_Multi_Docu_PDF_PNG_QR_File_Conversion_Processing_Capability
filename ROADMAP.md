# Roadmap

## v0.1 — local foundation

- Stable MCP v1 SDK over `stdio`.
- Explicit profiles and exact-tool selection.
- Real-path filesystem authorization and conservative limits.
- Token, context, RAG, CSV, JSON, QR, skills, tests, and documentation.

## v0.2 — common PDF operations

- Merge, split, rotate, organize, page numbering, metadata removal, and watermarking after Node-library and malformed-input evaluation.

## v0.3 — document and AI workflows

- PDF text/Markdown extraction, DOCX text/Markdown/HTML, and controlled local OCR with language, page, memory, and timeout limits.

## v0.4 — images and spreadsheets

- Image resize, conversion, compression, metadata handling, PDF/image conversion, CSV/XLSX conversion, and batch controls.

Dates are intentionally not promised. Security, deterministic behavior, cross-platform installation, and maintainability are release gates. Tool schemas may change during the `0.x` prerelease series.
